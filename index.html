Longest Bandana

🌹

priya,

Evathu nin huttu habha and evathu nangu habha. Evathu special dina. Full kushi. ❤️

Naavu phone alli mathadakke start madi 2 years agidhe namge gothe agila time yengoythu antha.

Nin nan life alli estu involve agidiya andre nanu ninna nenpu madkoldiro dina ne illa. Yengadru nenpu agtiya.

Nan bike alli ogbekadre nan inde kutiro thara, office inda mane barbekadre ninu mane alli nangoskara kaytirtiya antha anisutte.

Heege ninu nan jyothelli ediya. Yavaglu nodtirtiya.

Navvu night alli mathadidhu a preethi, dukha, kaneeru... yela thumba special adhu.

Yavdhu plan madi agidhu alla. Adagi adhe agidhu. I am just thankful for the memories.

♥ ♥ ♥

Ninge novvu madodu beda antha mariyakke, sayakke, dura agakke yela try madhe.

Meditation, gym, running, sports yella andre yela.

Nan estu try madidru ninu atra agtane bartidiya ortu. Dura agtila avagle artha aythu nin bittu nan kai erakke agalla antha.

Nange nidhe ne baralla kelvandu sathi. Manassu tumba heavy agutte. avaga nange nane hurt madkondini kai, kallu antha atleast a novige adru nidhe baruthe antha 😢

Preethi andmele challenges edhe eruthe. Adunella face madi jyothelli nediyode alva nijvada preethi andre.

Ethara avoid madi phone madhe message madhe yak erbeku? Adru badlu ebru kuthkondu okay yela problem agtha edhe e thought avrige yake banthu antha discuss madi solve madbodu alva.

Kasta no sukka no nadi nan jyothelli edini. Enadru parvagilla. Kone vusiru ero tanka jyothelli niltini anode alva Preethi en bandru give up madhe.

🌹
❤️
🌹

Elli yarigu hurt agalla kano. Just yelarigu nija gotila aste. Seriously nanu innu enmadbeku antha gotila.

E vaisalli monthly 1 lakh salary edhu vargu obru ondhu bet madi torsila. Infact yela.

Ethara hudga beku ano thara ne badkirodu. Innu en qualities beku hudga atra?

Okay just forgot about everything for a second.

Family avru evru yela bidu. Yaro madidh tappige atva nam grand parents thappige navvu yak bali agbeku?

Nanu ninu en tap madidivi dura aggakke?

Nodu already estondu time waste madidivi. Bedhe ero viysakke. kansu kanbeku maduve admele athara erana ethara erana antha fine but agantha evathu yak sacrifice madbeku?

Namge gotila nale ediyo ilvo. Yen bekadru agbodu. Atva corona thara innu enadru barbodu. So e dwesha kopa yela bittu evathu namdu antha yak baduktila navu?

Nodu evathu parents atva invabru hurt agbodhu by chance becuase truth gotila avrige.

But yochne madu. Next 50 years sacrifice madtiya? Yak bitkodtiya nin love? Yelaru nam jyothe estu dinna ertare helu.

End of the day nanu ninu ebre alva. So decision kuda nam ebru parvagi thagobeku alva?

Nodu nim parents ge dairyavagi helu. Nan yavathu neevu thale thagso antha kelsa madila.

Nan yenu tap madila neevu hudko antha atva adkinta best choice nan madirodu antha direct agi helu.

I promise you edunna nan vulskoltini, avre helbeku correct choice madidiya antha.

avru oppo vargu kayana artha madsana avrige, avru artha madkoltare. Adunna bittu call madhe message madhe erodhu estu sari nine yochne madu.

❤️

Nin ondhu message atva ondhu call nange thumba important. adu nan dinna mathu nan life ne change maduthe. Nan kansu thumba edhe.

But nan sumne edini enu madtila because nan heart helodu onde.

Avlu nin life ali illa andmele e sadne yak beku antha.

Ninu imagine kuda madalla nan nin estu love madtidini ninu estu mukya antha.

Ondhu ondhu second kuda kaytha edini ningoskara. Please artha madko.

Ninu nijvaglu ista patidre artha aguthe kayadu estu kasta antha.

Nine thane helidhu

Ninu nange beku. Nanu yenu change agalla inge ertini. Just nange job sigovargu time kodu antha.

Nanu ille tanka wait madhe tane. Evaga job madtidiya alva? Mathe yak inge madtidiya?

Just remind madu. Nodu nam preethi. Nan enella madidini heldange nedkontino ilvo anta eglu thumba kelsa.

But nin birthday nange estu special andre e website yela 2 weeks inda night 2 o clock vargu madini.edhu kasta alla nan kushi.

Nange gotila innu yen madbeku. Nan mele nambike barbeku andre ?

Ninge gotha ondhu ondhu sathi nanu outsider agi yochne maditini. Avaga ansuthe yathukoskara madtidiya edella antha.

Nange yavdhu cake walk erlila. Yela kade i fought ningoskara with parents, relatives etc.

Yen gotha nijvaglu intha hudgana kalkobeda life alli. Ondhu sathi miss adre mathe sigalla.

Yela helini. Hope artha agiruthe nan mele nambike, pretthi edhu yela artha agidre call madu.

Nanu phone munde ne kutirtini nin call goskara.

Invandu life ediyo ilvo gotila. Ee life alli bandu bidu Nan jyothe. already thumba kaydini.

Nin invabru jyothe maduve antha nenuskondre nan vusiru nin oguthe agalla nan kai alli.

Thumba helbitte ansuthe... Eglu nan kannalli neer thumbidhe... 😢
